    Agent responsibilities

    Proposed capabilities for AI callers integrated through the CRM

    Future integration
    • Qualify demand. Confirm location, property type, reported issue, urgency, preferred visit time and service-contact permission; write structured facts and a traceable summary to the CRM.
    • Coordinate the next step. Offer available appointments through an approved scheduling service, confirm callbacks and hand off uncertain or structural issues to a person.
    • Follow up on decisions. Remind a customer about an existing quote and record objections. Technical diagnosis, revised pricing and scope approval stay in the application’s authorized workflow.
    • Keep the customer informed. Explain the latest recorded milestone and create a support case when a concern appears. Do not infer completion or a warranty from a conversation.
    • Respect the customer’s choice. Honor contact eligibility and stop requests. Calls never approve homeowner START/CLOSE codes, evidence decisions or payment verification.

    More business questions to answer

    • Which sources generate approved jobs at the best acquisition cost?Requires real campaign spend and linked CRM attribution.
    • Which objections most often stall a quote?Record a reviewed objection category: budget, timing, scope, trust or competitor.
    • How long does a customer take to decide?Use first current-quote delivery and valid approval timestamps; report pending-age separately.
    • Where do booked visits fail to become inspections?Track attended, rescheduled, cancelled and no-show outcomes with reasons.

    A relationship beyond the handover

    Proposed CRM journeys after completion

    • 30 / 90-day care check. Ask whether the reported issue has returned; create a human-reviewed service case for concerns.
    • Pre-monsoon care. Contact customers with a recorded care preference and valid contact permission, then book an approved service.
    • Referral request. Offer a trackable referral link after a satisfactory outcome; measure resulting enquiries and approved jobs.
    • Repeat property work. Connect a returning customer to a distinct new site/job without merging property histories.
    • Service recovery. Prioritize unresolved concerns and low ratings before any promotional outreach.

    What the business should see

    • Follow-up completion and issue recurrenceObserved answers with timestamps, not an inferred durability score.
    • Care booking and completed-service conversionPreference → eligible contact → booking → completed service.
    • Referral-to-approved-job conversionRequires stable referral IDs and deduplicated customers.
    • Repeat-job value and retention cohortsRequires individual customer identities and mature observation windows.
    • Customer lifetime valueOnly after actual transaction history, costs and a clear definition are available.

    Minimum linked identifiers

    • CRM contact + site + opportunity IDsA person can own more than one property and more than one job.
    • Application workspace + lead + job IDsThe workspace ID is different from the visible PRO job code.
    • Call + appointment + task IDsConnect a conversation to a valid booking and later outcome.
    • Quote version + milestone event IDsPrevent old reminders, superseded approvals and duplicated outcomes.
    • Consent version + source attributionRecord permission source/purpose, opt-outs and campaign/referral lineage.

    Business definitions before launch

    • Qualified: service fit, supported location, confirmed need and a permitted next action.
    • AI-assisted: a qualifying AI contact precedes the outcome within an agreed attribution window. This is not proof of causal uplift.
    • Won: a valid homeowner-approved app quote. Completion and cash collection are later milestones.
    • Revenue: requires the organization’s accounting definition and verified source. Quote value alone is insufficient.
    • Successful booking: a confirmed slot accepted by the scheduling workflow, not a caller’s suggested time.
    Read this first: what this application is.ARDEX PRO is a connected local waterproofing-workflow prototype. It coordinates a homeowner, an applicator, a dealer and an operations reviewer using one persisted job record. It has two demo customer routes: Ananya’s ARDEX-sourced terrace enquiry and Meera’s applicator-owned bathroom job. This inventory describes the current repository; CRM and AI calling are proposed integrations.

    Orientation for an outside agent

    • UI entry points: main app at port 5173; Admin/Super Admin at port 5175/admin.html; service API at port 5174; public verification at passport.html?token=….
    • Main navigation: connected Experience selector; applicator Jobs, History, Earnings and Profile; admin Overview, Jobs, Review queue, Applicators, Dealers, Catalog, Rewards & revenue, Accounts, System, Pilot and Audit log.
    • Normal state progression: VISIT_BOOKED → DIAGNOSIS → QUOTE_SENT → APPROVED → KIT_READY → IN_PROGRESS → REVIEW/HANDOVER → COMPLETED. Cancellation and voiding are explicit exceptions.
    • Technology: React/TypeScript UI, custom CSS, Vite, Node HTTP service and local SQLite. IndexedDB stores active session selection; localStorage retains pending scan/photo submissions.
    • Authority: server-enforced role grants and domain rules decide what can be done; selecting another screen does not grant permission. Current shared demo accounts do not provide production customer isolation.
    • Important separation: customer approval freezes a quote; dealer readiness does not prove delivery; a scan does not prove application; closing confirmation does not issue a Passport; reported payment does not verify bank settlement.
    • Sources to inspect: src/ui/Workflow.tsx, GuidedPresentation.tsx, AdminApp.tsx, Operations.tsx; server/index.mjs, database.mjs; src/domain/*; config/catalog.json; docs/user-flows/*; PRODUCTION-HANDOFF.md.
    How to read these metrics
    • Every numeric dashboard value is computed from a deterministic fictional lead dataset embedded in this file. Filters use acquisition age relative to the sample snapshot, not live API data.
    • Funnel milestones are nested distinct-lead counts. Booking rate = booked ÷ qualified; qualification rate = qualified ÷ leads; approval rate = approved ÷ leads. A zero denominator displays N/A.
    • AI reach rate = distinct AI-reached leads ÷ distinct AI-attempted leads. Attempts count calls; reached counts leads. AI-assisted approval is a temporal attribution example, not a controlled uplift measure.
    • Selected-cohort outcomes reflect the latest fictional snapshot. The trend groups those leads by creation period; it is not an approval-event-time trend.
    • Open quoted value, approved scope, completed scope and acknowledged receipt overlap. Do not add them or call them recognized revenue.
    • Estimated care opportunity uses a stated ₹4,000 assumption per interested completed job. It is neither a frozen quote nor a receivable.
    • Live reporting needs mature cohorts, individual identities, linked CRM/app events and real timestamps. Exported values retain their illustrative label.
